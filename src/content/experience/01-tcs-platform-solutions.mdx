TCS Enterprise Manager is an internal IP asset under the Platform Solutions
unit — an enterprise platform in the same space as ServiceNow, covering ITSM,
ITIM, ITAM, APM and ITGSM.

I joined the team as a mobile developer and moved into a DevOps role when the
unit set out to modernise product delivery. Two of us were asked to take the
whole unit from SVN and manual, unreviewed deployments to a real CI/CD practice.

That meant designing the delivery model rather than just wiring tools together:
which branches exist and what they mean, what a merge has to prove before it
lands, how an environment is promoted, who is allowed to deploy where, and what
happens at 2am when a release goes wrong. The revert pipeline mattered as much
as the deploy pipeline — being able to undo a release in seconds is what made
the team comfortable releasing whenever they wanted to.
