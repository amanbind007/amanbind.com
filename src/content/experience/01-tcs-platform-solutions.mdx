TCS Enterprise Manager is an internal IP asset under the Platform Solutions
unit — an enterprise platform covering ITSM, ITIM, ITAM, APM and ITGSM.

I joined the team as a mobile developer and moved into a DevOps role when the
unit set out to modernise product delivery. Two of us were asked to take the
unit from SVN and manual deployments to a CI/CD model.

Much of the work was defining the delivery model before building anything:
what branches mean, what a merge has to pass, how environments are promoted,
who can deploy where, and what the rollback path looks like. The revert
pipeline ended up mattering as much as the deploy pipeline. Once a revert took
seconds rather than an hour, the team was willing to release outside the weekly
window.
