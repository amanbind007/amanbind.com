## Why breadth

When I moved from mobile into infrastructure I wanted coverage across the whole
surface rather than depth in one area — networking, identity, cost, resilience,
data and security posture. The study path forces that.

I worked through the architecture track first, then DevOps and operations. Each
one fed back into the platform work I was doing at the same time, particularly
around IAM and the deployment permission model.
