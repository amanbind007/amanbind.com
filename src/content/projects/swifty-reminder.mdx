My most-starred repository, which says something about how many people are
learning SwiftData by rebuilding Reminders.

Cloning a first-party Apple app is a good exercise precisely because the target
is unambiguous: you already know exactly how it is supposed to behave, so all
the difficulty lands on architecture and persistence rather than product design.
Supports light and dark appearance end to end.
