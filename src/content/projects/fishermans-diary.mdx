The most architecturally deliberate of my iOS apps. Two-way-binding MVVM with
Combine, SwiftData for offline persistence so catches and species information
stay available without a network, and SwiftSoup scraping the details out of
Fishbase.

The parts I would still defend: infinite scroll that loads fish data lazily
against both the search term and scroll position, and a deliberate separation
into dependency-injected repositories rather than view models reaching straight
for the network.

Built almost entirely from native iOS components, because an angler standing in
a river does not want to learn a bespoke interface.
