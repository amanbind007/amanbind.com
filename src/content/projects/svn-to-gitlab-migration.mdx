## Choosing the platform

The unit ran on SVN. Moving off it was a prerequisite for the pipeline work,
and the replacement had to be free and self-hostable to get through
procurement.

We installed, load-tested and scored the candidates against how the teams
actually work. GitLab CE won on self-hosting, CI integration and permission
model.

We ran the same exercise for project tracking, comparing Redmine and
OpenProject under load before standardising on OpenProject.

## Migration

History, branches and access mappings moved over per repository, alongside the
pipeline work landing in parallel.

## Rollout

The larger part was adoption. We published DevSecOps guidelines and process
documentation covering code review, scanning, deployment and access requests
under the new model, released the tooling, and ran knowledge-transfer sessions
for engineers and senior stakeholders.

I later helped several other Platform Solutions teams through the same
migration and worked with their DevOps engineers on pipeline and automation
design.
