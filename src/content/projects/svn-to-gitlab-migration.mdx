## Choosing the platform

The unit ran on SVN. Moving off it was a prerequisite for everything else we
wanted to do, but the choice had to survive procurement — it needed to be free
and self-hostable.

We ran a genuine evaluation rather than picking the popular answer: candidate
platforms were installed, loaded, stress-tested and scored against how the
teams actually work. GitLab CE won on self-hosting story, CI integration and
permission model.

We ran the same exercise for project tracking, comparing Redmine and
OpenProject under load before standardising on OpenProject.

## Migration

History, branches and access mappings moved over per repository, alongside the
pipeline work that was landing in parallel. Migration was the easy half.

## Making it stick

The harder half was the human one. We published DevSecOps guidelines and
process documentation covering how code review, scanning, deployment and access
requests work under the new model, released the tooling to the teams, and ran
knowledge-transfer sessions for engineers and senior stakeholders.

I later helped several other Platform Solutions teams through the same
migration and advised their DevOps engineers on pipeline and automation
architecture — that ended up being some of the most appreciated work I have done
at TCS.
