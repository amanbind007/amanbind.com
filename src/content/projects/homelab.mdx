## Why bother

Self-hosting is the cheapest way I know to build real operational intuition.
Running your own identity provider teaches you more about OIDC in a weekend than
a month of documentation, and being your own on-call for a service you actually
use makes you honest about backup strategy.

Most of what I have deployed at work started as something I broke at home first.

## Roughly what runs

- **Reverse proxy and TLS** — Caddy in front of everything, automatic certificates.
- **Identity** — Keycloak as a single sign-on provider across services.
- **Search and telemetry** — an OpenSearch instance, which is also where I prototyped the RCA correlation work.
- **Monitoring** — Prometheus and Grafana, same pattern as production, smaller blast radius.
- **Media and library services** — the workloads that make the rest worth maintaining.
- **Configuration** — Compose files and Ansible playbooks in version control, so a rebuild is a checkout away.
