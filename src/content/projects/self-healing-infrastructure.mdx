## Automated remediation

Once Prometheus and Grafana covered both infrastructure and application
metrics, we closed the loop on the alerts whose fix is always the same.

The main example: server disk usage crosses 70%, and instead of paging someone
the alert fires a webhook that triggers a Jenkins pipeline to prune the oldest
backups and clean the Jenkins workspace directories. The incident resolves
itself and leaves a record of what ran.

We automated the alerts whose runbook contained no decision, and left the rest
to a human.
